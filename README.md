# Playstore PowerBI Analysis

**Project Overview:**

The project focuses on analysis of Google Play Store application using Microsoft PowerBI to understand app distribution, user ratings, reviews, installation, pricing, app sizes, content ratings, and trends. The objective of analysis to identify patterns in app performance, comparing free and paid application, and evaluate how different app categories perform based upon user engagement and rating. 

**Project Objectives:**
- Analyse the distribution of free and paid applications.
- Identify the most common app categories and rating.
- Examine app size distribution and average app size across categories.
- Analyse app ratings by category, genre, and content rating.

**Data Cleaning:**
- Identify & handled missing and invalid values, in the data.
- Converted app sizes from MB to KB into a common numerical format.
- Cleaned numerical values such as reviews, installs, and price.
- Corrected data types for accurate calculations.

**Analysis Performed:**

- Calculated average app rating.
- Examine the number of unique app categories.
- Compared the distribution of free and paid apps. 
- Examined the app sizes.
- Identified the most common app categories.
- Analyzed the distribution of content rating.
- Evaluated application rating across different genre. 
- Identified applications with ratings 4.0 and above. 
- Examined application installation patterns. 
- Compared app rating and prices.

**Key Findings:**

- From the analysis, the most common type of application install was Free app which is 92.16%, and paid was 7.83%.
- In the data 6K apps are there which are rated with and above 4.0 ratings.
- Entertainment is the highest rating category compared to others which is 4.14 ratings.
